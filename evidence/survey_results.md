# Student Survey & Validation Notes: Placement & Internship Preparation

**Conducted**: October 2026  
**Target Group**: Engineering & College Students preparing for Internships and Placements  
**Sample Size**: 8 Respondents  

---

## 1. Survey Overview & Methodology

To validate the core problem statement for **Open Innovation (Student Pain Points)**, a structured survey and follow-up interviews were conducted with college students actively preparing for on-campus and off-campus technical placements and summer internships.

The goal was to determine:
1. What daily friction students experience while preparing.
2. Whether the lack of a structured, dynamic daily schedule causes anxiety and preparation stalls.
3. If students would adopt an intelligent, fatigue-aware study companion.

---

## 2. Quantitative Survey Findings

| Survey Question / Metric | Result (Count) | Percentage | Significance |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Currently preparing for internships or placements?** | 6 / 8 | **75.0%** | Clear majority actively in the preparation cycle |
| **Difficult to know which skills to improve?** | 6 / 8 | **75.0%** | Students suffer from lack of objective skill-gap feedback |
| **Lack a clear, structured daily preparation plan?** | 7 / 8 | **87.5%** | **Core Pain Point**: Daily execution stalls without clear direction |
| **Juggle multiple fragmented websites / resources?** | 5 / 8 | **62.5%** | Context switching across LeetCode, YouTube, PDFs, college portals |
| **Difficult to decide what to study next tonight?** | 7 / 8 | **87.5%** | **High Cognitive Friction**: Decision fatigue wastes initial study momentum |
| **Would adopt a tool with skill gaps + daily plan?** | 6 / 8 | **75.0%** | Strong demand and willingness to use |
| **Rated proposed tool 5/5 for usefulness?** | 4 / 8 | **50.0%** | Half gave the highest possible rating, with remaining at 4/5 |

## 4. Qualitative Student Interview Quotes

During 1-on-1 interview debriefs, students shared their real daily frustrations:

> *"After 7 hours of college lectures and lab files, I sit at my desk at 7 PM wanting to prepare for placements, but I spend 40 minutes just deciding whether to do DSA, OS, or finish my lab record. By then, my brain is tired and I end up doing nothing."*  
> — **3rd Year B.Tech CSE Student (Tier-2 Engineering College)**

> *"Existing to-do apps like Notion or Todoist are dumb. If I get an emergency assignment from college, Notion just keeps showing overdue red badges. It doesn't tell me what placement topic I can skip or swap without ruining my streak."*  
> — **Final Year Information Technology Student**

> *"I have 20 tabs open: Striver's sheet, LeetCode, GFG, college LMS, and YouTube. When a task says 'Solve Graph DFS', it feels so huge that I procrastinate. If a tool broke that down into 10-minute micro-steps, I would start immediately."*  
> — **Pre-final Year Student preparing for Summer Internships**

---

## 5. Conclusion & Product Validation

The survey provides indisputable primary evidence:
1. **Decision Fatigue is Real**: 87.5% of students struggle to decide what to study next each night.
2. **Current Tools Fall Short**: Generic calendars and to-do lists do not adapt when college coursework clashes with placement prep.
3. **High Product Market Fit**: 75% of respondents confirmed they would immediately adopt an AI tool that identifies skill gaps and provides an adaptable daily preparation plan.
