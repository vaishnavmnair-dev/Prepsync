> **Hackathon Submission Dossier**  
> **Track**: Problem Statement 4 — Open Innovation (Student Pain Points)  
> **Focus**: Placement Preparation Struggles, Decision Paralysis, and Dynamic Daily Planning  
> **Survey Scope**: 8 Engineering & University Students across 2nd, 3rd, and 4th Years  
> **Validation Status**: High Product-Market Need (75% Willingness to Adopt, 50% Top 5/5 Rating)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Research Objective

To rigorously validate the student pain point before architecting **PrepPilot (AdaptiveStudy AI)**, we conducted primary research through a structured survey and follow-up qualitative debriefs with college students actively preparing for technical internships and campus placements.

### Primary Research Goals:
1. **Validate Decision Paralysis**: Measure how much cognitive friction students experience when deciding what to study every evening.
2. **Quantify Planning Deficiency**: Determine the percentage of students who lack a concrete, adaptive daily preparation roadmap.
3. **Evaluate Resource Fragmentation**: Understand how context-switching across fragmented websites (LeetCode, LMS, YouTube, PDFs) hurts consistency.
4. **Assess Product Demand**: Test whether students would adopt an intelligent companion that diagnoses skill gaps and provides fatigue-aware daily guidance.

## 5. Qualitative Debriefs & Real Student Voices

During 1-on-1 qualitative interview debriefs, students elaborated on their day-to-day pain:

> *"I come back to my hostel room after college at 6:30 PM. I want to prepare for placements, but I have 25 tabs open—LeetCode, Striver sheet, YouTube, and college lecture PDFs. I spend 40 minutes just debating whether I should do Linked Lists or finish tomorrow's DBMS assignment. By then, my willpower is gone and I open Instagram."*  
> — **3rd Year B.Tech CSE Student**

> *"Normal to-do apps like Notion don't help because they don't understand that college assignments take real time. If I have a sudden lab exam tomorrow, Notion just marks all my DSA tasks as overdue red badges. It makes me feel like a failure instead of adjusting my schedule."*  
> — **Final Year Information Technology Student**

> *"When an item on my list says 'Solve 3 Hard Dynamic Programming problems', it feels like a mountain. If an app broke that into 10-minute starter steps with code hints, I would start right away."*  
> — **2nd Year Student preparing for Summer Internships**

## 7. Conclusion & Judging Takeaways

1. **The Problem is Real & Urgent**: College students are under immense placement pressure, but their daily execution collapses due to decision fatigue and unrealistic scheduling.
2. **Existing Tools Are Insufficient**: Generic calendar apps cause anxiety through rigid deadlines, while practice portals offer thousands of questions without daily pacing.
3. **High Willingness to Adopt**: With **75% adoption intent** and a **50% top usefulness rating**, the data proves that **PrepPilot** meets a real, unserved student need.
