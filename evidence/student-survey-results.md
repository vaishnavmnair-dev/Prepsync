# Student Survey Results

## Survey Overview

A short survey was conducted to understand the problems students face
while preparing for internships and placements.

Total respondents: 8 students

## Key Findings

- 75% (6/8) students are currently preparing for internships or placements.
- 75% (6/8) students find it difficult to know which skills they should improve.
- 87.5% (7/8) students do not have a clear daily preparation plan.
- 62.5% (5/8) students use multiple websites/resources for preparation.
- 87.5% (7/8) students find it difficult to decide what to study next.
- 75% (6/8) students would use a tool that identifies skill gaps and provides a daily preparation plan.
- 50% (4/8) students rated the proposed tool 5/5 for usefulness.

## Biggest Problems Reported

- Time management: 37.5% (3 students)
- Coding / DSA: 25% (2 students)
- Lack of proper guidance: 25% (2 students)
- Other: 12.5% (1 student)

## Conclusion

The survey indicates that students face real difficulties in organizing
their placement preparation and deciding what to study next. Most
respondents lack a clear daily preparation plan and want a tool that
can identify their skill gaps and provide personalized daily guidance.

The survey provides evidence that the identified student problem is
real and worth solving.
